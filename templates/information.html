ℹ️ About PoseGuard

PoseGuard is an advanced AI-powered driver monitoring system designed to enhance road safety through real-time detection of unsafe driver behaviours. Using your vehicle's cabin camera, PoseGuard continuously analyses the driver's pose and posture, instantly identifying dangerous actions such as phone use, distracted driving, drowsiness, and more.

Built on state-of-the-art computer vision and deep learning techniques, PoseGuard leverages a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) trained on thousands of real-world driving scenarios. The model classifies driver behaviour into 8 distinct categories with high accuracy, enabling proactive intervention before incidents escalate.

PoseGuard's intelligent alert system includes voice announcements via text-to-speech technology, real-time screenshot capture of unsafe events, and automatic video clip logging. All incidents are stored securely in MongoDB with structured metadata including event type, confidence score, timestamp, and driver identity.

Designed for fleet operators, transport companies, and safety-conscious individuals, PoseGuard provides a scalable, cloud-ready solution that integrates seamlessly with existing vehicle monitoring infrastructure. The admin portal gives safety managers complete visibility into driver behaviour patterns and incident history.
